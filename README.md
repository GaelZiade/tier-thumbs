# Tier Thumbs

Batch-make square thumbnails with a name bar, for TierMaker.

**Live: https://gaelziade.github.io/tier-thumbs/**

Drop a pile of character images, and each one comes out square with a black bar and
its name in white along the bottom. Everything runs in the browser — your images are
never uploaded anywhere.

## What it does

- **Batch load** — button, drag & drop, or paste from the clipboard. Any size, any aspect ratio.
- **Names from file names** — `geralt_of_rivia.png` becomes `GERALT OF RIVIA`. Edit any of them
  under its thumbnail, or paste a whole list (one per line) with **Paste names…**.
- **Always square** — the image sits above the bar (nothing covered), or the bar overlays it.
- **Crop** — fill (crop) or fit whole. Default anchor top / center / bottom, so full-body art
  keeps the face. Drag a thumbnail to move the crop, scroll on it to zoom.
- **Text that fits** — long names shrink, then wrap to two lines.
- **Style** — bar height, bar and text color, bar opacity, font, text size, uppercase, outline,
  background color or transparent.
- **Export** — 128 to 1024 px, one PNG per character in a `.zip`, named after the character.
  Single PNGs per card too.

Settings are remembered in the browser; images are not.
